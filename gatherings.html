BEFORE YOU PACK

A few good
things to know.

How close is the gondola?

The listing describes an approximately 900-foot walk to the River Run Gondola and Village. Bring comfortable shoes for the short walk.

Where will everyone sleep?

A king bedroom, a queen bedroom and a fold-away Murphy bed give your group room to settle in. The condo accommodates up to six overnight guests, with two full bathrooms.

Is parking included?

One space in the heated underground garage is included. An elevator serves the building. Additional vehicles need separate parking arrangements.

Are the pool and hot tubs private?

The heated pool, two hot tubs, steam room, fitness center and game room are shared Red Hawk Lodge amenities, subject to building access and maintenance.

Can we bring pets?

The current house rules do not allow pets or smoking. Please review the full rules and current conditions before reserving.
